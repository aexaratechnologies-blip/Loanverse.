Our Financial Services

Funding and financial advisory solutions for businesses, entrepreneurs and individuals across India.

Working Capital Finance

Finance for inventory, supplier payments, payroll and receivables gaps.

Business Loans

Funding for eligible businesses to expand, hire, open locations, purchase inventory or meet defined business needs.

Project Funding

Funding structured around a defined project’s cost, implementation schedule, approvals, promoter contribution, projected cash flows and execution risks.

Machinery & Equipment Finance

Finance for eligible plant, machinery and productive equipment.

Loan Against Property

A secured facility against eligible residential or commercial property.

OD/CC & Restructuring

Overdraft and cash-credit facilities provide revolving working-capital access within sanctioned limits.

Debt & Equity Funding

Debt and equity options are evaluated against capital needs, cash flows, leverage and willingness to dilute ownership.

Startup & Growth Funding

Capital routes for eligible early-stage and scaling businesses, including debt, equity, incubator programmes and eligible public schemes, based on traction, stage and financial plan..

Pre-IPO & Investment Funding

Pre-listing capital planning, investor readiness and investment structuring coordination with qualified intermediaries.

Sector-Specific & Structured Finance

Tailored funding assessment based on an industry’s assets, contracts, cash flows and risks, including manufacturing, infrastructure, real estate, renewable energy and logistics..

Retail Loans

Loan options for eligible individuals and small-business borrowers.

Government Schemes

Guidance to identify potentially relevant government-backed credit, guarantee or subsidy programmes.

IPO Advisory

IPO readiness, governance and financial preparation, documentation planning and coordination with qualified intermediaries.

One-Time Settlement (OTS)

Support to organise case information and facilitate discussions for a negotiated settlement of outstanding dues.

Equity Financing

Capital raised by offering an ownership stake to investors.

Bill Discounting

Access to cash before eligible invoices or trade bills reach maturity, subject to financier review of the transaction, buyer risk and invoice validity..

Export Finance

Funding support for export businesses, potentially covering pre-shipment needs, post-shipment receivables and export-related banking facilities, subject to eligibility..

Debt Syndication

Coordination of large borrowing requirements across multiple lenders, including funding structure preparation, lender information and discussions on indicative terms..

Government Grants

Help identifying relevant grant programmes and organising applications against published criteria.

Supply Chain Finance

Solutions that help buyers and suppliers manage payment timing and working capital through eligible invoices or approved payables and participating financiers..
